## Research Planning
## 1. Research Objective
The purpose of the this research is to look into how to best illustrate the challenges that GMU CS Students face
when planning their remaining courses and choosing electives that align with their career interests. 
The research will help identify requirements for PathForge, our course planning system that is designed to connect
academic progress with career goals.

## 2. Current Student Experience
GMU currently provides Degree Audit, a tool which allows students to be able to view their academic progress, completed reqs,
and remaining credits needed for graduation. However, I have seen that Degree Audit can be difficult to navigate and interpret.
On top of that, it does not suggest the clear courses that would align with my career interests.

As a result, students may need to independently research course descriptions, prerequisites, and degree requirements before 
deciding which courses to take.

## 3. Identified User Problems
Based on the observation, there are potential issues that have been identified.
- Unclear course options: Students may struggle to determine which courses satisfy their degree requirements, but also 
support their career interests.
- Limited career guidance: Existing information on degree planning does not clearly connect the course options to career paths.
- Confusing user experience: Students may have difficulty understanding the their remaining requirements and available choices.
- Fragmented information: Students may need to consult multiple resources to understand degree requirements, course descriptions, 
and career relevance.

## 4. Student Needs
The observations above suggest that students benefit from a system that:
- Identifies which courses contribute toward their degree and their careers
- Provides understandable information about available courses and prerequisites
- Help students identify courses relevant to their intended career fields
- Reduces the amount of manual research when needing to plan out their future semesters

## 5. Implications for PathForge
These findings motivate PathForge Student Profile Features.
The proposed feature would allow students to provide their completed courses and select their career interest in one place
An additional proposed capability is importing academic information from an uploaded transcript or using Degree Audit.
Student would review and correct the extracted information before saving it to their profiles.
This profile would then provide PathForge would the information needed for course recommendation and degree planning functionalities.
